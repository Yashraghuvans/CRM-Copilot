X200 router: factory reset procedure

Hold the recessed reset button for 10 seconds until the power LED blinks amber. Release and wait two minutes for the unit to reboot.

After reset, log in at 192.168.1.1 with the default credentials printed on the base label and reconfigure the WAN settings (PPPoE or DHCP, per the customer's ISP sheet).

If the router reboots randomly after reset, suspect the power supply. Swap the adapter before replacing the unit.
